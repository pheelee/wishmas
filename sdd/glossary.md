# Glossary

## Terms

- **Household language:** The language selected during setup and stored in `household.yaml`. It applies to everyone; after setup it is changed only through YAML.
- **Setup display language:** The initially browser-detected language used throughout setup, independently of the household language selected for use after Finish setup.
- **Supported locales:** Swiss Standard German (`de-CH`, informal address) and American English (`en-US`).
